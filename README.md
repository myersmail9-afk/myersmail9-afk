# Joseph Myers

I build AI and data tools for an arborist company in Utah. I direct Claude Code, which writes the code, and then I test what it builds before it goes out to the crew or to customers. One of those tools tells a homeowner how much to water a tree, using live local weather and a published USU Extension method.

Before this I was CEO of a nonprofit that ran literacy and vocational classes for 500+ children a year in Herat, Afghanistan. We had 15 staff there and I relied heavily on our local managers. I also lived in southern Africa for almost two years, in Namibia, Botswana and South Africa, and I loved it.

The two concept demos below use only public data. Every number links to its source, and neither one is an official product of the group it's about.
