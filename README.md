# Joseph Myers

I build AI and data tools for an arborist company in Utah. I direct Claude Code, which writes the code, and then I test what it builds before it goes out to the crew or to customers. Most of that work lives in the company's own private repos, so it doesn't show up here.

Before this I was CEO of a nonprofit that ran literacy and vocational classes for 500+ children a year in Herat, Afghanistan. We had 15 staff there and I relied heavily on our local managers. I also lived in southern Africa for almost two years, in Namibia, Botswana and South Africa, and I loved it.

The concept demos below use only public data. Every number links to its source, and none of them is an official product of the group it's about.

- [giraffe-id](https://myersmail9-afk.github.io/client-demos/giraffe-id/): a working app that tells individual giraffes apart by their coat spots, flags possible injuries for a vet to review, and keeps an audited record for each animal (3-minute guided tour)
- [Peace Parks concept demo](https://myersmail9-afk.github.io/peace-parks-concept/)
- [Buffelsfontein baseline](https://myersmail9-afk.github.io/buffelsfontein-baseline/)
- [Kunene rain and fire](https://myersmail9-afk.github.io/client-demos/kunene-rain-fire/): every wet season since 1991/92 and NASA fire detections for Namibia's Kunene region
- [Camera-trap reporting pipeline](https://myersmail9-afk.github.io/client-demos/valais-wolf-pipeline/): a repeatable, checked report from public camera-trap data (Valais wolves)
- [Namibia giraffe records](https://myersmail9-afk.github.io/client-demos/giraffe-namibia/): every public giraffe record for Namibia, cleaned, mapped and linked to the original
- [What the cameras saw](https://myersmail9-afk.github.io/client-demos/snapshot-safari-sa/): Snapshot Safari South Africa camera-trap counts, site by site
